01 Overview

Style-preserving
teacher distillation

WEBKD trains web agents using text-only corrections from a black-box teacher. The teacher identifies the first error in a student trajectory and edits the remaining responses at their original contexts.

Corrections preserve the student’s reasoning style while changing incorrect decisions.

Example correction
OBSERVATION
[15] link ‘Simple Shop’
[31] textbox ‘Search’
[47] button ‘Search’
Think

I need to search for “bookshelf”. The search box is 31, so I’ll type there.

Plan

1. Type bookshelf → 2. Search → 3. Compare prices.

Actionfill('31', 'bookshelf')

The action is corrected, while the short, direct phrasing stays close to the student’s style.

02 The method

Method

P1 / INITIALIZE→
T→S

Behavior cloning

Behavior-clone successful teacher demonstrations to give the student its initial web skills.

P2 / EXPERIENCE→
12×

Student rollouts

Collect its own trajectories. Failed attempts become edit targets; successes provide examples of its style.

P3 / LOCALIZE→
✓✓×

Error localization

The teacher identifies the earliest error that sends the task off course. Earlier correct steps stay intact.

P4 / CORRECT↗
S+✓

Style-preserving edits

Correct each response in the faulty suffix at the student’s original context, using its writing as a guide.

Unpack the contribution scores, weight map, and anchor
Eq. 5 / Training objective

Weighted imitation and rationale anchoring

Weighted imitation
Emphasize reasoning spans that support the corrected action. Action and step tokens receive full imitation weight.
Rationale anchor
Limit drift from the frozen seed on reasoning tokens. Action and step tokens are almost unanchored.

Each term is normalized by its own weight sum; β controls the anchor strength.

Eq. 2 / The mismatch

Perplexity premium

This is a difference in log perplexities, not raw perplexities. Uniform training can spend its largest loss on unfamiliar wording instead of the useful decision.

Eq. 4 / Credit assignment

Leave-one-out action contribution

Both scores use the frozen behavior-cloned student. A span earns credit when its presence increases the probability of the corrected action. Negative contributions stay in the ranking.

Eq. 10 / From rank to weight

Within-step rank weights

Ranks are normalized within each step; ties share their average rank. With φ = 0.1 and τ = 0.05 nats, identical contributions yield 0.55. Action and step CE weights are pinned to 1.

Eq. 5 / How far to move

Role-asymmetric anchoring

Here pᵢ is the current student’s target-token probability and qᵢ is the frozen seed’s. On corpus target tokens, use γ = 1 for think/plan/memory and γ = 10⁻⁶ elsewhere. This pointwise surrogate is not an exact full-vocabulary KL. β = 0.03 in the reported configuration.
